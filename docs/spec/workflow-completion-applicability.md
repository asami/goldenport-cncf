## R1 — Actual result/Continuation scope, not diagnostic equality

and model scope, typed result, required facts/evidence and execution disposition.
Workspace/evidence snapshots are diagnostic information, not unchanged-content
certificates. Recovered claims resolve Required SPI/Operation/type and completion
contracts without reconstructing a complete WorkOrder and comparing its body.
Original saved Context meaning/presentation remains input; it is not replaced.

incompatible Operation/stale revision, one-shot resume and duplicate rejection.

## R2 — Serialize once; consume actual committed state/result

their own output as a certificate. V1/V2 readers retain their declared structure,
types and required references, without whole canonical-byte comparison. Public
completion removes repeated issue lookup and selected-current body certification.
Actual selected adjacent entry/instance target is admitted; storage resolves its
current active revision/suspension and uses established CAS and one-shot status.
Authoritative transaction readback decodes actual committed instance,
Continuation and completion at their native representation/reference boundaries.
It does not recertify the planned state, revision or result/closing reference
after the guarded provider has committed them. Managed response
consumes that committed outcome without another full-instance comparison.
Malformed readback remains indeterminate; it never replays work or fabricates
verified publication. Owner/collections/same-provider scope, local effect/event
policy, rollback and permissions remain unchanged.
