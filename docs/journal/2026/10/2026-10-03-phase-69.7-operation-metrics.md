## Frozen alternatives and chosen boundary

The parent chose a separate completed-result recorder rather than extending the
existing fourteen-fact/56-cell lifecycle taxonomy: emitted native facts and
returned public results mean different things. It chose strict identical-result
wrappers with final-engine private submit-body extraction. Wrapping both original
public overloads without extraction would double count delegated successful
two-argument submissions. The chosen extraction preserves both original lifetime
and quiesce preflights, original default-option getter/flatMap order and the whole
three-argument native body/effect/error-release expression. It makes no new ABI,
Product, schema, override-dispatch or security decision.

queue native work and emit its Event, then fail its zero-timeout wait. Successful
hidden/missing/None readers remain returned. Control keeps authorization before
shape and lookup; selected Reader calls keep the by-name snapshot and original
shape/cursor/filter/caller/snapshot/offset/authorization order. No clock, IDs, user
callback, body, copied model, provider or metric compensating effect is introduced.

operation-second order rather than a new State lock or global atomicity claim.

fresh State starts zero and bootstrap/shutdown neither reset nor reconstruct it.
