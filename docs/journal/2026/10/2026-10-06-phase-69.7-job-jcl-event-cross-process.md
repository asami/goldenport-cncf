identity, UoW termination and stagePostCommit effects are separate from wrapper
outcome. Failed commands are not recorded as committed; later wrapper failure
cannot erase an already committed source delegate. The original event observer
records ReceptionDomainEvent and context between source and handlers, and the
writer reads actual EventRecords for id/after/name/kind/payload/time/lineage,

global deduplication or external delivery is claimed.

authenticated terminal/payment facts; positive typed native JobEntity debug is
read under the original lease. Inline submissions have no saved-definition

management remains UnavailableAfterRestart, and foreign policy is preserved.
Native row/payment baselines remain unchanged; reader actions, commits, source
requests/pairs, recovery port, Blob I/O/publications, SQL/Entity writes, events
and redispatch are zero. Ephemeral/failure readers receive no ID and assert
the empty durable namespace without manufactured failed terminal recovery.
